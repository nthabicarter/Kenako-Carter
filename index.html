A Letter From Mom

I can't believe it's been 16 years since the day I first held you in my arms. It feels like it was just yesterday.

You were in such a rush to enter this world and meet me, arriving with those big, beautiful eyes wide open and giving scaring everyone at the hospital! Little did I know that from that very moment, my life would change forever in the most beautiful way.

You are my first child, the one who made me a mother. Because of you, I have grown into a better person and a better mom.

You have taught me how to be selfless, shown me what true love really means, and taught me patience in ways I never imagined. Because of you, I am stronger. Because of you, I have learned to love in a way I never knew was possible.

God knew exactly what He was doing when He blessed me with you. He knew I needed a best friend, someone I would love unconditionally for the rest of my life.

Being your mom has been one of the greatest blessings of my life, and loving you has been the easiest and most beautiful thing I have ever done.

Nana, you are beautiful inside and out. You have such a loving heart, and you are an amazing daughter and a wonderful big sister. Yes, you can be a little mean sometimes, LOL! But even then, you are still the most wonderful daughter, sister and I wouldn't change a thing about you.

I am so incredibly proud to be your mom. I am proud of the person you are today and even more excited to see the beautiful, strong, and amazing young woman you are becoming.

As you turn 16, I want you to always remember one thing: You are loved beyond words. You are appreciated, you are valued, and you are so incredibly special. Not just today, but every second, every minute, and every single day of your life.

Never doubt how much you mean to me.

Thank you for being such a wonderful daughter. Thank you for all the laughter, the memories, the lessons, and the love you have brought into my life.

Most importantly, thank you for being YOU.

Never change who you are to please the world. Always stay true to yourself, follow your dreams, and remember that no matter how old you get, you will always be my baby girl.

I love you with every piece of my heart, more than words could ever explain.

Happy Sweet 16, my Nana! I hope your special day was everything you dreamed of and as sweet and beautiful as you are.

I love you forever and always, my angel. ❤️

Happy 16th Birthday, Kenako Nadia Selah Carter! 🎂🦋💕
